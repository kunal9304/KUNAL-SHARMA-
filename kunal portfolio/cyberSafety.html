Cyber Safety Tips
for Students

Essential practices for staying safe in the digital world.

🔒 Password Safety

Use strong, unique passwords for every account. Combine uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Use a password manager to stay organised.

🎣 Phishing Awareness

Never click links from unknown senders. Verify the sender's email address carefully and don't share personal info via email.

🌐 Safe Browsing

Always look for HTTPS in the URL bar. Avoid downloading files from untrusted sites and keep your browser updated.

📱 Social Media Privacy

Keep your accounts private. Think twice before sharing location or personal details publicly online.

💬 OTP & Scam Awareness

Never share your OTP with anyone — not even someone claiming to be from a bank or company. Real organisations never ask for it.

🛡️ Device Security

Keep your OS and apps updated. Use antivirus software and lock your device with a strong PIN or biometric lock.
